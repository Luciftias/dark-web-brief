ART · RECONSTRUCTION

This page is a reconstruction.

On August 4, 2013, every site hosted by Freedom Hosting served a maintenance page almost exactly like the one you just saw. The page was not maintenance. It was a browser exploit — CVE-2013-1680, a use-after-free in Firefox 17 ESR — and it beaconed visitors' real IP addresses to a server in Virginia.

The codename, from leaked training material: EgotisticalGiraffe. Tor was not broken. The browser was. Its operator, Eric Eoin Marques, was sentenced to 27 years in 2021.

What this teaches: a fake maintenance notice is one of the oldest tricks in the record — and every broken URL on this site rebuilds one, labeled as art, because a museum and a trap should look different.
